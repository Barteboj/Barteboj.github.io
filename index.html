About

A decade of shipping games

I'm an experienced Unity Developer with a decade of proven success solving complex problems, delivering high-quality results and collaborating across teams. Many productions have been released with my significant contribution.

My work spans the full stack of game development: core gameplay mechanics, advanced AI systems, responsive UI, and robust networking. I've engineered multiplayer and netcode with Photon, built an automated tournament system that ran unattended on a cloud machine and defined the backend contract it ran against, optimised game logic and graphics for stable framerates, and ported titles across Valve, Meta, Pico and WMR headsets as well as desktop and mobile.

Beyond features, I care about the codebase itself — refactoring legacy systems for maintainability, establishing internal programming standards, and taking part in intensive code reviews to spread knowledge across the team.

I worked 4 years in an office and 6 years remotely, and I do well in both. Game development has been my dream profession for many years, and I still approach the work with passion — I want to give my all to create the best productions.
